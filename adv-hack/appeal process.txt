In CPGRAMS, Nodal Public Grievance (PG) Officers are designated officials appointed in every Ministry, Department, and State/UT to oversee, categorize, and monitor the resolution of public grievances, while the Nodal Authority for Appeal (Nodal Appellate Authority) is a senior officer appointed to handle and dispose of appeals filed by citizens who are unsatisfied with the initial grievance resolution. [1, 2]  
Nodal Public Grievance Officers 

• Role: Responsible for supervisory oversight of Grievance Redressal Officers (GROs), root cause analysis, monitoring pendency, and examining citizen feedback on the  Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System . 
• Appointment: Every Central Ministry, Department, and State Government appoints dedicated Nodal PG Officers to ensure fair and time-bound handling of complaints. 
• Directory: Specific names and designations vary by department and can be checked directly on the CPGRAMS Nodal PG Officers List. [3]  

Nodal Authority for Appeal (Appellate Authority) 

• Role: Empowered to review the processes and guidelines followed by Grievance Resolution Officers and dispose of formal citizen appeals within 30 days. 
• Appointment: An officer senior in rank to the Nodal Grievance Resolution Officer is designated as the Nodal Appellate Authority. They are also authorized to appoint Sub-Nodal Appellate Authorities when necessary. 
• Trigger: The appeal option to this authority is enabled on the portal when a citizen registers a "Poor" feedback rating after a grievance is closed. [4]
